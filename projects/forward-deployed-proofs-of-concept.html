Needs-assessment assistant

HPHS

The need

An AI assistant for needs assessment, built for HPHS and the client teams it works with.

What I did

Became its technical lead in the second half of 2025 when a teammate left. Supported sprint planning and prioritization, turned business needs into technical specifications, managed the infrastructure as code for its AI Labs and hybrid cloud deployments, and stayed responsive to customer bugs.

The result

Completed the production handoff to HPHS's development team in 2026, with documentation, onboarding, knowledge transfer and a reusable playbook, so the client's own team can run it.

Django 5 · Next.js 16 · Azure OpenAI · PostgreSQL · Pulumi

Distributed policy simulation

Regulatory Policy & Economics

The need

A public-policy case study needed a Monte Carlo simulation across tens of thousands of scenarios, work that had been bought from an outside subcontractor.

What I did

Rebuilt the simulation workflow in R and Python and ran it on AWS SageMaker, using ScriptProcessor to spread the work and writing results to S3. Also ported the team's geospatial analysis workflow to SageMaker.

The result

Replaced a paid external subcontractor with in-house capability that is cheaper and faster. The Europe and Asia team wanted to reuse the approach.

R · Python · AWS SageMaker · S3

Sales forecasting

ICF sales and executive leadership

The need

ICF's leaders needed forward-looking estimates of sales and revenue.

What I did

With a colleague, built win-probability, quarter-close and revenue models in R. Built the Azure Data Factory pipeline that feeds them into Power BI, including the connection from the sales database to Azure storage. Presented sales-to-revenue and book-to-bill analysis to the executive leadership team.

The result

The C-suite and executive management use it regularly for financial forecasting.

R · autoXGBoost · Azure Data Factory · Power BI

Account-based marketing models

Sightline

The need

Utilities hold customer data scattered across many systems. ICF's Sightline platform turns it into account-based marketing for commercial and industrial customers.

What I did

Built generative-AI models that predict a business customer's type and parent company. Because the data was sensitive, I compared small local language models (DeBERTa, F-Net, BERT-tiny) inside a secure analytics enclave with frontier models.

The result

My part of the Sightline work that won the Gold Award for B2B Data Excellence at the Association of National Advertisers' 2026 B2 Awards. I was one contributor on a larger team.

Python · DeBERTa · F-Net · BERT-tiny · frontier LLMs
