AI survey platform

A survey that runs as a conversation. An AI agent asks the questions, a form appears inside the chat when a slider or ranking is clearer than text, and fixed rules decide what comes next.

My role
Lead; primary author
Years
2026
Built with
Python, OpenAI Agents SDK, React, PostgreSQL
Built for
ICF's Enterprise AI Strategy initiative

The problem

ICF wanted to know how far AI-assisted coding had spread among its developers: who uses which tools, how well, and what is in the way. A static form gets thin answers, and a free-form chatbot gets answers that are hard to compare.

What I built

A conversational survey that keeps the structure of a form. The agent handles the conversation, and the survey definition handles everything that must be consistent.

Design choices

  • The conversation is flexible; the survey is not. Questions, branching and wording live in a versioned schema, so every respondent is measured the same way.
  • Schemas are validated and stored unchanged, so a result can always be traced to the exact survey version that produced it.
  • Controls such as sliders and ranking appear inside the conversation, which gets cleaner answers than asking people to type numbers.

Outcome

  • Used to measure AI-assisted coding adoption, skills and tool readiness across ICF's developer population.
  • A conversational survey that stays consistent from one respondent to the next.
